Trained and evaluated both architectures on MELD and DailyDialog across five random seeds.
Produced prediction files, performance metrics, confidence intervals, and stable-versus-shift analyses.
Prepared an executed notebook and saved-model demonstration interface.
Created presentation slides, figures, narration script, and recording to explain the methods and findings.
