Designed and implemented ShiftAware (workstream #6): per-speaker and conversation memories, causal retrieval over other speakers' turns, a speaker prior, current evidence, a shift head and a learned gate that mixes the two emotion distributions.
Trained it end to end with RoBERTa on MELD and DailyDialog over five seeds, selected variants on dev only, and ran exploratory transformer and EmoWOZ variants.
Evaluated it against the RoBERTa baselines with the shared metrics, stable/shift strata and paired dialogue-bootstrap tests (H3: shift-turn target not met).
Prepared the executed notebook, prediction CSVs, saved model for the demo, slides, figures and narration script.
